Krillion Infinite

Krillion Infinite is a free endless word game you play in the browser. There is no signup, no download, and no timer pressuring you between rounds. You get a prompt, you type one answer, and the rarer your answer, the deeper you dive.

Play Krillion Infinite free

How it works

Each dive gives you 7 prompts with 25 seconds each. Answers are scored by rarity, from Plankton at 10 meters for the most common answers up to One in a Krillion at 100 meters for answers almost nobody thinks of. A perfect dive reaches 7,000 meters. Your depth meter sinks through five ocean zones as you play, from Sunlight down to the Hadal zone.

What you get

  • Unlimited play — endless dives, as many as you want, whenever you want.
  • A new daily dive every day at midnight local time, with past dives you can replay.
  • Daily answers and hints published for every date, so you can check what you missed.
  • A guest leaderboard — no account needed, your best dives are remembered on your device.
  • Practice packs of themed prompts when you want to sharpen up on a topic.

Why rare answers win

Most word games reward the first answer that comes to mind. Krillion Infinite rewards the opposite: the answer nobody else thinks of. That turns every prompt into a small creative puzzle. "Name a fruit" is easy. Naming a fruit nobody else names is the game.
